Journal quests (game data): "Thirst for Power" (journal tag Lv 30; it completes once you've hunted 3 more V Bloods
and equipped a spell, which most players do around Lv 20–30) rewards the Research Desk;
"Getting Ready for the Hunt" (Lv 20: Sawmill, Simple Workbench, raise Gear Level) rewards Blood Tracking;
"Broaden Horizons" (Lv 40) asks you to unlock Iron Smelting and explore beyond Farbane Woods;
"Lord of the Night" (Lv 90) is defeating Dracula.
