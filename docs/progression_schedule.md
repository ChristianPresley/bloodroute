# Progression schedule: brand-new character → endgame caster

This takes a fresh vampire to the endgame build in `pve_spellcaster_build.md`, in the order the game opens things up.

**Sources:**
- **Boss unlocks:** each V Blood's technology rewards in the vrising.gaming.tools data (`vblood_rewards.md`, patch 1.1.13), cross-checked against the wiki.
- **Spell points and Veil drops:** `research_sources.md`.
- **Recipes:** the scraped item data.
- **Map links:** [Map Genie's V Rising map](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran). Every 📍 opens that boss, place or resource on the map. Map Genie's popup *reward* text is out of date, so trust this schedule for rewards.

**How to read it:**
- **Boss levels** are V Blood levels. Fight at a **Gear Level at or above the boss level**: you deal about 4% less damage per level under (and about 1% more per level over).
- **The V Blood menu** tracks bosses, but it blurs ones far above your Gear Level.
- **Spell points:** every V Blood gives one Spell Point for a fixed school and tier; spend it in the Spellbook on any spell of that tier.
- **Spell School Mastery:** traits unlock as you *spend* 3 / 5 / 7 points in a school. The build wants **Chaos 7** and **Illusion 3**.
- **★** marks a boss the build needs.

## Phase 1: Wake up (start → Lv 20) · Farbane Woods

**Goal:** a castle, bone gear, Chaos Volley and the crossbow line.

**Opening quests** (the journal walks you through these):
1. Leave the crypt and collect bones. You get the **Bone Sword** and **Bone Ring** (GL 3, +5.3 Spell Power, 32 Bone).
2. Craft the **Boneguard** set from the inventory.
3. Gather wood and stone, then place a **Castle Heart** on a [castle plot](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6117), plus a coffin, chest and Mist Brazier.
   - The **"Shelter"** quest unlocks the Furnace, Sawmill, Simple Workbench, Grinder and **Blood Press**.
   - The Furnace smelts **Copper Ingot** from [Copper Ore](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6091).
4. Build a Sawmill and Simple Workbench and raise your Gear Level. This unlocks **Blood Tracking**.
5. Upgrade to **Plated Boneguard** at the Simple Workbench.

**Starting kit:** Shadowbolt and Blood Rite are known from the start. Equip **Shadowbolt + Blood Rite** and use your starting dash as the Veil.

**Bosses:**

| Lv | Boss | Map | Take |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | Alpha the White Wolf | [📍](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178446) (roams a circuit of Farbane roads around the Wolf Den) | Wolf Form (fast travel). 25% chance of an Unsullied Heart |
| 20 | ★ **Errol the Stonebreaker** | [📍](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178451) | Chaos T1 point → **Chaos Volley**. Equip **Chaos Volley + Shadowbolt** |
| 20 | ★ **Keely the Frost Archer** | [📍](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178445) | **Tannery, Leather**, **Empty Waterskin** (for Enchanted Brew). Frost T1 point → Cold Snap (defensive) |
| 20 | ★ **Rufus the Foreman** | [📍](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178449) | **Woodworking Bench** and the whole **crossbow line** (Bone → Copper → Iron → Dark Silver). Blood T1 point → Blood Rage |

**Unsullied Hearts:** these early bosses (Alpha, Errol, Keely, Rufus) each have a 25% chance to drop one. Keep every heart: you'll need 4 for the ring in Phase 3 (and 1 more to summon Nibbles, if you want Rat Form).

**Craft:**
- **Bone Crossbow → Reinforced Bone Crossbow → Copper Crossbow.** Copper takes 16 Copper Ingot + 8 Plank at the Simple Workbench.
- **Nightstalker set** (Simple Workbench; vest = 8 Leather + 4 Coarse Thread). It's the base for the Warlock set.
  - Before the Loom (Lv 40), **Coarse Thread** comes from looting [Farbane bandit camps](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178215,178231,178234,178238,178242,178243,178245,178259,178372,178373,178374,178375,178376,178379,178380,178381,178382,178383,178388,178390,178392,178527) or the Shady Goods Dealer at the [Shady Merchants Camp](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=283054,284512) (no Human Form needed).

**Blood:** feed on **Scholar** carriers: Nuns, Priests, Villagers, Alchemists, Witches, Devoted. Scholar stays your blood until late game.

## Phase 2: First tools (Lv 20 → 30) · Farbane Woods

**Goal:** research, Warlock armor, and the first mastery points.

**Research Desk:** the journal quest **"Thirst for Power"** (tagged Lv 30) completes as soon as you've beaten 3 more V Bloods and equipped a new spell. For most players that's in this phase. It rewards the **Research Desk**; build it.

| Lv | Boss | Map | Take |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27 | ★ **Grayson the Armourer** | [📍](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178453) | **Whetstone** (Merciless Copper weapons), **target dummies** (use one for the endgame bolt test). Illusion T1 point → **Phantom Aegis** (Illusion point 1 of 3) |
| 27 | ★ **Goreswine the Ravager** | [📍](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=178447) (roams the forest) | **Gravedigger Ring**, Grave Dust. Unholy T1 point → **Bone Explosion** |

**Craft:**
- **Gravedigger Ring** (GL 9, +9.7 Spell Power): 12 Grave Dust + 32 [Mourning Lily](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6116). Wear it until Phase 3.
- **Unsullied Hearts:** Grayson to Gaius (Lv 27–55) each drop one guaranteed, except Alpha, Errol, Keely, Rufus, Nibbles, Lidia, Kodia and Finn (25%). From Lv 53 on most V Bloods drop one 80% of the time and some none (game data drop tables), but by then Tristan's recipe makes Greater Blood Essence without hearts.
- **Research Desk blueprints** are a random draw within a category: 60 Paper for a weapon or ring, 50 for armour or a brew. Each armour draw is one random piece out of 16 (four sets).
- **Research Desk recipes:**
  - **Merciless Copper Crossbow** (GL 12): Copper Crossbow + 2 Crude Topaz + 12 Whetstone + 4 Leather.
  - **Warlock Vestment.** Each piece = a Nightstalker piece + 4 Leather + 4 Coarse Thread + 8 Copper Ingot. All 4 pieces give +7.2% Bonus Spell Power; the set bonus is 2-piece +4% cooldown, 4-piece +1 Gear Level.
- **Castle Heart level 2:** 12 Leather + 12 Copper Ingot.

**Loadout:** starting dash · **Chaos Volley + Shadowbolt** (or Bone Explosion) · no ultimate yet.

**Research currency:** Paper, Scrolls and Schematics drop from humans (Paper in Farbane, Scrolls in Dunley, Schematics in Silverlight, the Cursed Forest, Oakveil and Gloomrot North) and chests, and the Devourer turns spare blueprints into them. Nicholaus's Paper Press also crafts all three (4 Scrolls from 4 Coarse Thread + 8 Paper; 12 Schematics from 40 Tech Scrap + 16 Scrolls), so kill him if drops run short.

**Craft:**
- **Ring of the Sorcerer** (GL 12, +12.8 Spell Power; Research Desk recipe):
  1. Craft a **Greater Blood Essence** at the **Blood Press** from Unsullied Hearts (the wiki lists 4; check the count at the press).
  2. Combine Gravedigger Ring + 4 Crude Amethyst ([gem nodes](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6098)) + 1 Greater Blood Essence.
  - With Grayson's and Goreswine's guaranteed hearts plus this phase's bosses, you'll have 4 hearts early in Phase 3.
- **Enchanted Brew** (+3 Spell Power, Alchemy Table): 32 [Snow Flower](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6090) + Fish Bone + Empty Waterskin.
- **Vermin Salve / Blood Rose Brew** for healing ([Blood Rose](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6088)).
- **Iron Crossbow** (Smithy, after Quincey): 12 Iron Ingot + 8 Plank. Mining [Iron Ore](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?catIds=6093) needs a GL 12+ weapon.

**Early build, complete (≈33 DPS):**
- **Abilities:** Veil of Blood · Chaos Volley + **Bone Explosion** or **Shadowbolt** · Chaos Barrage.
- **Gear:** Warlock Vestment, Ring of the Sorcerer, Iron Crossbow.
- **Blood and potion:** Scholar 100%, Enchanted Brew.
- **Spell 2:** Bone Explosion's lead (33.4 vs 32.7) comes mostly from short fights; at 10 minutes it's 31.6 vs 31.4.
- **Rotation:** Volley → spell 2 → Veil; ultimate when both spells are on cooldown.
- **Leaving Farbane:** the journal's "Broaden Horizons" (Lv 40) sends you to **Dunley Farmlands**.

**Prisoners and 100% blood:**
1. **Get Dominate** through the journal chain:
   - "Waygate" gives the Stone Coffin.
   - "Lord of the Manor" (bind to a Stone Coffin) gives the Servant Coffin.
   - "Servants" (build a Blood Press and Servant Coffin) gives **Dominate**.
2. **Find a high-quality carrier.** Tristan's **Blood Hunger** shows blood type and quality over enemies' heads. Look for a **high-quality Scholar carrier** (Nuns, Priests).
3. **Dominate it and lock it in a Prison Cell.** Feed on the prisoner to take its blood, or drain **Blood Potions** into Empty Glass Bottles to carry.
4. **Push quality toward 100% later.** Corrupted Fish raises a prisoner's quality (±2% per feed), but it's only caught in **Oakveil** or at **Brighthaven Docks** (Silverlight Hills). Until Phase 6, catch a prisoner that's already high quality.

**Craft:**
- **Hollowfang Battlegear** (Tailoring Bench; chest = 12 Cotton Yarn + 8 Wool Thread). **Keep wearing Warlock**: Hollowfang is weaker and is only the base for Dark Magus.
  - Before Beatrice and Christina unlock the recipes, Cotton Yarn and Wool Thread can be looted in Dunley.
  - [Cotton patches](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=432218,432220,432221,432222) are in Dunley.
  - The [Dunley Farmers Market](https://mapgenie.io/v-rising/maps/vardoran?locationIds=355902) also sells them, but needs Human Form (Bane, Lv 50).
- **Scourgestone Pendant** (GL 15, Artisan Table): 8 Scourgestone + 24 Gem Dust.
- **Castle Heart level 3:** 8 Reinforced Plank + 24 Glass + 1 Greater Blood Essence.
  - Completing it ("Reign Supreme") gives the **Eye of Mortium**, which tracks Rift Incursions (needed for shards from Phase 5 on).
  - Build the Eye inside your territory: 4 Iron Ingot + 4 Scourgestone + 20 Scroll.
- **Study:** spend Scrolls on **Dark Magus**, **Merciless Iron Crossbow** and **Pendant of the Sorcerer** (90 for a weapon or pendant, 75 for armour, each a random draw within its category). Their materials arrive in Phase 5.

**Loadout from Kriig on:** Veil of Blood · **Chaos Volley + Unholy Chains** · Chaos Barrage.
- **Unholy Chains:** it channels for up to 1.7 s and the tether breaks if you get more than 14 m from the target. Don't start it if your Veil is back within ~2 s.
- **Gear:** Warlock Vestment, **Scourgestone Pendant** (16.5 Spell Power, GL 15, over the ring's 12.8 and GL 12), Iron Crossbow.
- **Elixir and potion:** Prowler once Meredith is down; keep drinking Enchanted Brew.
- **DPS:** with Veil of Blood this loadout tops out around **102** even with full Lv 70 gear (less in this phase). With the same Lv 70 gear it reaches ~115 once Veil of Chaos replaces Veil of Blood (Phase 5).

**Stygian passives:**
- **Stygian Shards** come from **Tier 1 Rift Incursions** (recommended Lv 57+; every 30 min, tracked with the Eye of Mortium) and Ruins of Mortium points of interest.
- The Altar's **Discover** unlocks a **random** elemental passive for 400 Stygian Shards. Awakening Scrolls from Rift V Bloods unlock a chosen one.
- Target **Enhanced Conductivity, Renewing Flames**, then **Chaos Kindling** when Cyril's third slot opens (and Cold Soul for Phase 7).
- Each Discover gives a random passive you don't have yet, out of 12. Landing all four takes about 10.4 Discovers on average (k specific out of 12 take k·13/(k+1)), so ≈4,160 Stygian Shards. The Altar itself costs 1 Regular Ruby + 12 Iron Ingot + 4 Greater Blood Essence.

**Mid build at Lv 60 (≈91 DPS):** two passive slots (Enhanced Conductivity + Renewing Flames), Regular jewels, Chaos mastery still T1.
- **Abilities:** **Veil of Chaos · Lightning Tendrils + Unholy Chains · Chaos Barrage**.
- **Gear:** Dark Magus, Pendant of the Sorcerer, Merciless Iron Crossbow.
- **Blood, elixir, potion:** Scholar 100%, Prowler, Enchanted Brew.
- **Rotation:** Tendrils → Veil → Chains; ultimate when both spells are down. Veil → primary attack → recast.

**Choose your endgame build** (full detail in the build guide):
1. **Wear the Soul Shard of Dracula.** It works with any ultimate.
2. **Run the test in build A's full setup:** **Mutant 100% + Elixir of the Blasphemous + the shard**. Without Blasphemous the cut is ~13 s and A looks worse than it is. Use a Grayson target dummy:
   - Time Blood Storm's cooldown without Veiling. ~120 s means Ultimate Cooldown Rate doesn't speed the timer; ~79 s means it does.
   - Veil once and land the follow-up attack. Watch how far the cooldown jumps: ~14.5 s is the default assumption. Two separate ~7 s jumps (Veil attack, then recast + attack) is also good for A.
   - Count how many of the 25 bolts hit.
3. **Use A if the Veil cycle cuts ≥ ~14 s (one big jump or two ~7 s jumps) *and* ≥ ~90% of bolts land.** Otherwise pick by your bosses:
   - Bosses teleport or break the Chains tether → **A2**.
   - Tendrils' bolts also miss → **A3**.
   - Many Blood Storm bolts miss → **B+**.
   - Unsure → **A2**.

**Mutant blood for A:**
